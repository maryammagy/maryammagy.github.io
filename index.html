PUBLISHED RESEARCH

Scientific Reports29 May 2023

Removing the association of random gene sets and survival time in cancers with positive random bias using fixed-point gene set

Maryam Maghsoudi, Rosa Aghdam & Changiz Eslahchi

Developed a fixed-point gene-set method to reduce spurious associations between gene expression and cancer survival. Across 17 TCGA cancer types, the approach removed positive random bias in 8 and reduced it in the remaining 9.
